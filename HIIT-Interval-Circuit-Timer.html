I bought Pro, but the app shows Free

Open the ⋯ menu, tap HIIT Timer Pro, then tap Restore purchase. Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to buy Pro.

I can't hear the voice cues

Check that the speaker button in the app isn't showing an X, and that your phone's volume is up. The cues play even when the ring/silent switch is set to silent.

The voice sounds robotic

Download a more natural voice on your iPhone: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English, then pick a Premium or Enhanced voice such as Ava or Evan. Reopen the app and choose it under ⋯ → Coach voice.

Will I lose my routines if I get a new phone?

Routines and history are stored only on your phone. They move to a new iPhone if you restore it from an iCloud or computer backup of your old one. Pro can always be restored for free with Restore purchase.

Privacy policy

Effective November 6, 2026

HIIT Interval Circuit Timer ("the app") is made by John Thompson. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: the app does not collect any personal data.

What the app stores

The app saves your routines, your workout history from the last 30 days, your chosen coach voice and your mute setting. All of this is stored only on your iPhone. None of it is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

What the app does not do

  • It has no accounts or sign-in.
  • It does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, health data or device identifiers.
  • It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • It does not sell or share any information.

Voice cues

Spoken cues are produced by your iPhone's built-in text-to-speech. The words are spoken on the device and are not sent to the developer.

Purchases

HIIT Timer Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. The developer never sees your payment details. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's own privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete routines in the app, clear history from the History tab, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. Because nothing is kept anywhere else, there is nothing further to request.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
